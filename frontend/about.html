LifeLink Mission

We bridge the communication gap between local blood donors, active patients, and emergency rooms during clinical crises.

Standard blood drives and bank structures are slow and geographically static. In an emergency hemorrhage, surgery trauma, or urgent treatment need, every minute counts. Clinical & Platform Guidelines

According to clinical safety regulations, a mandatory cooldown buffer of 90 days (3 months) is required between whole blood donations. This ensures the donor's hemoglobin and iron indexes completely recover.

Our smart recommendation algorithm scores donor suitability on a scale of 0 to 100 based on four clinical weights:
• Clinical Compatibility (40%): Blood compatibility matches (exact vs compatible).
• Proximity distance (30%): Distance computed using the Haversine formula from coordinates.
• Eligibility check (10%): Verification of the 90-day rest cooldown criteria.
• Historical response speed (20%): Historical response time history recorded by the donor.

O Negative (O-) red blood cells lack the A, B, and Rh antigen proteins. This means O- blood can be safely transfused to patients of any blood group without causing severe adverse immune reactions. It is highly valued as the universal donor in critical trauma cases where the patient's blood type is unknown.
